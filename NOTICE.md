# Third-party sources

This repository distributes original experimental observations and derived results. It does not redistribute the source cartography or third-party software used in the study.

The evaluation used the following external resources. Their copyright and licensing terms remain with their respective providers:

- Natural Earth Ocean, minor-island coastline, and reef layers
- Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database (GSHHG)
- SeaRoute
- The author-released implementation associated with Funke et al. (IJCAI 2024)
- Euclidean Hub Labeling author implementation

Exact versions and scientific roles are recorded in [`00_documentation/data_sources.csv`](00_documentation/data_sources.csv). The associated manuscript and supplementary material provide the full bibliographic citations and methodological context.
